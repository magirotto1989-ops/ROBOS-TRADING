## 2. CHANNEL POSITIONING

### The Blue Ocean Formula
Most WWII channels pick ONE lane:
- Weapons-only (Forgotten Weapons, Tank Encyclopedia)
- Pure battles (Mark Felton Productions)
- Strategy only (TIK History)

**Your edge: Cross-category "revelation" content.**
Every video answers the implicit question: *"What did you NOT know about this?"*

### Positioning Statement
> "The WWII channel that exposes the decisions, secrets, and turning points that mainstream history ignores."

### Differentiators
1. **Moral complexity** — don't just cover what happened; cover WHY leaders made the choices they did
2. **Counterfactual hooks** — pair every major event with a "what if" angle in the title/thumbnail
3. **Human-scale stories** — zoom in on single soldiers/officers inside big battles for emotional resonance
4. **Speed** — tie content to anniversaries, news hooks (WWII museum openings, film releases, declassified documents)

## 6. PRODUCTION STACK (Faceless)

### Visuals
- **Archival footage:** Internet Archive (public domain), Prelinger Archives, British Pathé (YouTube embed)
- **Maps & graphics:** Mapchart.net → After Effects / CapCut for animation
- **AI visuals:** Midjourney / RunwayML for scene reconstructions (clearly labeled as AI)
- **Stock footage:** Artgrid, Storyblocks (subscription)

### Audio
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs (AI voice — choose "Daniel" or custom voice clone)
  - Alternatively: Hire narrator from Voice123 / Voices.com for premium feel
- **Music:** Artlist, Epidemic Sound — search tags: "cinematic war", "dramatic orchestral", "tense documentary"
- **Sound effects:** Freesound.org, Soundsnap

### Editing
- DaVinci Resolve (free) or Premiere Pro
- Color grade: desaturated, high contrast, sepia tones for archival feel

### Script → Video Pipeline
1. Research (ChatGPT + primary sources)
2. Script writing (800–1,400 words per 10 min video)
3. Voiceover recording/generation
4. B-roll assembly
5. Map animations
6. Music layering
7. Thumbnail creation (Canva Pro / Photoshop)
