# 90-Day Content Calendar — WWII Channel Launch

---

## LAUNCH STRATEGY PHILOSOPHY

**Weeks 1–4 (SEO Foundation):** Upload 3x/week. Focus on evergreen, high-search-volume topics. Goal: build catalog and watch hours for YPP qualification.

**Weeks 5–8 (Authority Building):** Drop to 2–3x/week. Introduce more complex, differentiated content. Build email list.

**Weeks 9–12 (Virality Push):** 2x/week. Release 2–3 viral-formula videos designed for homepage/browse traffic. Test thumbnail styles aggressively.

### Week 1 (Days 1–7) — LAUNCH WEEK

**Day 1 (Launch) — Monday**
- **Title:** `The REAL Reason Hitler Stopped the Panzer Advance at Dunkirk`
- **Pillar:** Battles
- **Why first?** "Why Hitler stopped at Dunkirk" is one of the most Googled WWII questions. Strong SEO from day one.
- **Thumbnail concept:** Split image — Panzer tanks on left / Allied soldiers evacuating on right. Text: "HE STOPPED"

**Day 3 — Wednesday**

- **Pillar:** Battles
- **Why?** Stalingrad = highest search volume of any WWII topic. This is your SEO anchor video.
