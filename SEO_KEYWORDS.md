# SEO Keywords & Metadata Strategy — WWII Channel

---

## YOUTUBE SEO FUNDAMENTALS FOR HISTORY CHANNELS

YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine. For a WWII channel, **60–70% of views in Year 1 will come from search**, not recommendations. This makes SEO the most critical growth lever at launch.

### The 3 Metadata Levers
1. **Title** — Highest SEO weight. Must include primary keyword within first 5 words.
2. **Description** — First 2 lines shown in search results. Include keyword 2–3x naturally.
3. **Tags** — Lower weight than before, but still important for "suggested video" placement.

## CHAPTERS STRATEGY

YouTube chapters (timestamps in description) improve:
- Average view duration (+8–15%)
- Search snippet visibility (chapters appear in Google search)
- User experience and rewatch rate

### Chapter Formula for WWII Documentary (10–15 min video)
```
0:00 — Hook / Cold Open
1:30 — Context & Setting the Stage  
3:00 — [Main Subject Begins]
6:00 — [Turning Point / Key Revelation]
9:00 — [Climax / Consequences]
12:00 — Legacy & Historical Significance
13:30 — Conclusion
```

**Rule:** Never name a chapter something generic like "Introduction." Use dramatic, curiosity-generating chapter titles:
- Bad: `Background`
- Good: `The Decision That Changed Everything`
- Bad: `The Battle`
- Good: `Six Hours That Shocked the World`

## POSTING TIME OPTIMIZATION

### Best Days to Post (History Niche)
1. **Thursday** — Viewers search over the weekend; Thursday posts peak Fri-Sun
2. **Saturday morning** — Direct Saturday search traffic
3. **Sunday morning** — Pre-commute browsing, high engagement

### Best Times (Viewer's Local Time)
- **8:00 AM – 11:00 AM** — Morning browse session
- **7:00 PM – 9:00 PM** — Evening wind-down

### Anniversary Hook Calendar
Post videos timed to WWII anniversaries for massive organic traffic spikes:

| Date | Anniversary | Video Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 27 | Liberation of Auschwitz | Holocaust content, "The Final Days of Auschwitz" |
| Feb 19 | Iwo Jima landing (1945) | Iwo Jima battle content |
| Apr 25 | Liberation of Dachau | German POW camp content |
| May 8 | VE Day | "The Last Days of Nazi Germany" |
| Jun 6 | D-Day | D-Day content (biggest traffic day of year for WWII channels) |
| Aug 6 | Hiroshima bomb | Atomic bomb content |
| Aug 15 | VJ Day | End of Pacific War content |
| Sep 1 | Invasion of Poland | Operation Barbarossa, start of war content |
| Dec 7 | Pearl Harbor | Pearl Harbor content (second biggest traffic day) |

**Rule:** Have D-Day content ready for June 6 EVERY year. It is the single highest-traffic day for WWII content on YouTube.

## TITLE SEO CHECKLIST

Before publishing every title, verify:

- [ ] Primary keyword appears in first 5 words OR in a highly visible position
- [ ] Title is 50–65 characters (mobile-optimized)
- [ ] Contains at least one power word (REAL, Secret, Hidden, Worst, Greatest, etc.)
- [ ] Creates a curiosity gap or implies a revelation
- [ ] Does not duplicate an existing video title exactly
- [ ] Has been checked in TubeBuddy/VidIQ for competition level
- [ ] Thumbnail text complements but does not repeat the title verbatim
